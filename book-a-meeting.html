Book an Impact Review

The first of two conversations in your Impact Review.

This session is diagnostic, not a pitch. We will look at where your organisation may be under pressure across people, governance and capacity, and whether there is anything worth examining more closely. Nothing is recommended without both conversations completing first.

Whether or not further work follows, you will leave with a clearer picture of your current arrangements and what, if anything, they may be costing your mission.

Duration 30 minutes
Format Video or phone
With Matthew Steiner, Aetas Impact
Cost No cost or obligation beyond the conversation itself
