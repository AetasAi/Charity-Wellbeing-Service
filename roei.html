Return on Employee Investment for Charities

The commercial framework behind every Charity Performance Audit.

Charities invest significantly in their people — in salaries, pensions, recruitment and staff support. Mission delivery depends on that investment performing. The Aetas ROEI Framework helps charity leaders measure whether it is, and improve it where it is not.

ROEI in a charity context

Every pound your charity invests in people should work as hard as possible.

Charities face a particular challenge. Staff retention, volunteer engagement, limited budgets and the constant pressure of mission delivery all create a uniquely demanding environment. When financial pressure affects staff focus, absence rises, quality of work falls and mission delivery suffers — but the connection is rarely visible to leadership until the cost is already significant.

The ROEI Framework gives charity leaders and trustees a structured way to measure and improve the return on their investment in people — demonstrating responsible governance and protecting the organisation's capacity to deliver.

The ROEI Framework

Five stages. One repeatable methodology.

Every Aetas for Charities engagement follows this framework. It is adapted to recognise the specific governance requirements and budget constraints of charity organisations.

Stage 1

Measure

Understand how effectively your current investment in people is performing.

  • ✔Employee benefits utilisation
  • ✔Pension engagement levels
  • ✔Financial wellbeing indicators
  • ✔Workforce demographics and life stage
  • ✔Employee communication effectiveness
  • ✔Benefits spend per head

Stage 2

Diagnose

Identify where the gaps are and what they are costing the organisation.

  • ✔Financial stress and presenteeism
  • ✔Poor benefit engagement
  • ✔Avoidable staff turnover
  • ✔Recruitment cost pressure
  • ✔Productivity and focus
  • ✔Retirement and succession planning gaps

Stage 3

Recommend

Practical initiatives with a clear commercial rationale — nothing introduced without one.

  • ✔Financial wellbeing programmes
  • ✔Employee benefits review
  • ✔Pension education and engagement
  • ✔Life stage financial planning
  • ✔One-to-one financial guidance
  • ✔Communication strategy

Stage 4

Implement

Aetas coordinates delivery through trusted partners. Full visibility without managing providers.

  • ✔Managed delivery across all agreed services
  • ✔Employee communications handled
  • ✔Provider coordination and oversight
  • ✔Progress reporting throughout

Stage 5

Measure Again

ROEI is a continuous cycle, not a one-off exercise.

  • ✔Engagement and retention metrics
  • ✔Benefits utilisation rates
  • ✔Employee financial confidence
  • ✔Absence and productivity data
  • ✔Organisational performance review
